# World v4 fixtures

Loaded by `fixtures.spec.ts` (every `*.json` here must migrate and validate, and load unchanged) and by `world-model`'s `devices.spec.ts` (each passes the load check against the built-in catalogue). `schemaVersion` 4 is provisional until v0.5.0: these may change with it, in the same PR; after that they are frozen.

- `empty.json`, `leads-only.json`, `routed-wire-hot-terminations.json`, `cable-one-unlanded.json`, `mounted-devices.json`, `float-edges.json`: the v3 fixtures of the same names as the v3 → v4 step migrates them (no areas, every entity unplugged, every connection untaped, the 12 V lamp in `leads-only.json` with `burnedOut: false` and `overvoltageSinceTick: null`). See `../../v3/world/README.md` for what each holds.
- `house-relations.json`: what world v4 adds. A duplex receptacle with a kettle in its `top` outlet and a toaster in its `bottom` one, a lamp plugged into a power strip, two conductors with re-identification tape (a white taped black at both ends, a black taped red at end `b`), and three areas (`basement-east`, `bathroom`, `kitchen`, sorted by id; the bathroom and kitchen share a wall face, which the half-open boxes keep apart).
- `protection-state.json`: the protection state ADR 0019 persists, at tick 41 200: a 24-space load centre (`enclosure.fixture-load-centre-24`) with part of its directory filled (spaces 1, 2, 3 and 5; every other `directory1` … `directory42` key `null`), a plug-on breaker part-heated inside its magnetic opening window (`magneticSinceTick` set), a GFCI breaker whose `residualIntegral` reached 1 inside its mechanism time, a tripped AFCI (`arcFault`, `arcExposure` and `arcLastSeenTick`), a two-pole breaker with one bimetal per pole, a tripped GFCI receptacle (`groundFault`, unlatched, its press counters; `residualIntegral` 0 and `residualSinceTick` `null`, since the integral resets below the pickup), a burned-out 120 V lamp, a hair dryer plugged into the GFCI, a three-way switch and a dimmer.

- `load-centre.json`: the load centre's kinds (P4 PR 14a, ADR 0019's P4 PR 14 amendment), built with the world commands on world-model's TEST-ONLY entries (`LOAD_CENTRE_REFS`): a 20-space load centre (`panel.test-lc-20`) with its whole directory filled (`directory1` … `directory20`; `directory21` … `directory42` `null`), its dead front on and one knockout open; a two-pole main breaker on `mainFeed` (handle on); one- and two-pole branch breakers on both bus columns; a main bonding jumper on `mbj` and a ground bar kit on `groundBar`. The refs are test-only (the load check here leaves them alone; `world-model`'s `load-centre.spec.ts` runs the full load check against the test entries) until PR 14 ships the real entries.

**The receptacle, appliance, breaker, load-centre, switch and dimmer refs are fixture-only** (`*.fixture-*`): their kinds and entries arrive in later P4 PRs, so the load check leaves them alone, and their key sets are provisional until ADR 0021 (this file is replaced in place when they settle). The lamp refs are real catalogue entries, so their bags are checked. `protection-state.json`'s load centre still holds the 45-key bag with an `on` key that the kind never got (ADR 0019's P4 PR 14 amendment: 44 keys, no `on`, `coverOn` starting `false`); it keeps its fixture-only refs until PR 14 ships real load-centre and breaker entries, which replace them in place.

`../refused/world/` holds one document per new schema invariant (ADR 0019 invariants 9–11) that the loader must refuse, each listed with its message in `fixtures.spec.ts` (`REFUSED_V4`). `../ctrl/` holds this build's engine-7, catalogue-3 scenario saves (rebuilt from their scenarios by `apps/sim-cli/src/sources.spec.ts`) and `house-relations.ctrl` (`house-relations.json` with an empty log).
